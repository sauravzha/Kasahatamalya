Demonstration Approach

Transforming Schools From Within

We embed ourselves directly inside government schools and demonstrate what excellent, joyful, holistic education looks like — then help the system replicate it.

Rajasthan

School Excellence Program

The Problem: Government schools often lack dedicated support to transition from rote-learning environments to holistic, child-centered spaces.

Our Response: We embed dedicated facilitators directly inside government schools to systematically transition them into self-sustaining hubs of quality learning, student well-being, and structured institutional governance.

C.E.L.L.
Cluster Excellence Learning Labs — our on-ground model

Teacher Support Program

The Problem: Teachers in underserved government schools face burnout, lack pedagogical support, and have limited access to mental health or professional development resources.

Our Response: A targeted, peer-led professional development model that equips public school educators with the precise instructional toolkits and mental health frameworks needed to manage complex classrooms effectively.

Includes SEE Learning (Social, Emotional & Ethical Learning) curriculum — developed in partnership with Emory University & the Dalai Lama Trust.

Community Leadership Approach

Learning Reaches Home

We mobilize local youth and communities to take learning directly to children's doorsteps — bridging the gap between school and home.

Gogunda, Rajasthan

Fale Fale Shiksha Muhim

The Problem: In remote tribal areas, children often drop out of school or fall behind in foundational literacy — unable to access quality support even within communities.

Our Response: A localized, doorstep-delivery learning model that maps, trains, and deploys neighborhood youth to bridge foundational literacy deficits right inside children's homes — going door to door ("Fale Fale" means "all around" in local dialect).

All Regions

Learning Festivals Internship

The Problem: Children from underserved communities rarely get exposure to creative, project-based, experiential learning that builds practical reasoning and self-expression.

Our Response: A short-term immersion track where outside talent (college students, young professionals) pairs with local youth to run intensive, project-based learning camps focused on creativity, peer learning, and self-expression.

Running since June 2017. Reached multiple locations across all three states.

Systems Excellence Approach

Building Permanent Systems

We organize communities, parents, and institutions to create self-sustaining systems of educational oversight and support that don't depend on our presence.

Adopted by MCD · All Regions

STAR Parents

The Problem: School Management Committees (SMCs) exist on paper but rarely function effectively — leaving communities without a voice in their children's education.

Our Response: A structural intervention that organizes and coaches parents to actively run School Management Committees, establishing a permanent system of civic oversight and at-home learning routines. Now adopted by MCD Delhi as a model program.
