The Child at the Center

Our Approach

A 3-Pronged Approach

After 10 years of working across 3 geographies, Kshamtalaya has arrived at a 3-Pronged approach to inch closer to our vision of Holistic Growth of Child through Learning, Well-Being and Governance.

1

Demonstrate & Support Excellence

in Learning, Governance & Well-being

  • 100 Schools of Excellence within primary grade government schools
  • Increased child retention
  • Improved quality in learning & well-being
  • Increase in parental engagement in PTMs
  • Print-rich & learner centric classrooms
2

Cultivating Local Leadership

  • Cultivating a cadre of local Edu leaders as Community Mentors who support govt. schools builds foundational skills in primary grade students belonging to remote rural geographies
  • Cadre of govt. school teachers who are transformed to happy, purposeful & improved pedagogical leadership
  • Cadre of Learning Festival Community Leaders supporting students develop creative confidence and enhance their 21st century life skills through access to art, music, theatre, movement, game design and other art related mediums.
3

Building Systemic Excellence

in Policy & Reforms

  • Supporting the state in the mission of NEP 2020's State Curriculum Framework
  • Advocating curriculum development & building a cadre of 100 Mentor Teachers for well-being curriculum to build happy schools.
  • Partnering with state to support Assessment CELL initiatives & Module Development for Teacher Education
What We Do

Our Programs

Five interconnected programs working across three approaches — Demonstration, Community Leadership, and Systems Excellence — all anchoring the child at the center.

Demonstration Approach

School Excellence Program

Rajasthan

We embed dedicated facilitators directly inside government schools to systematically transition them into self-sustaining hubs of quality learning, student well-being, and structured institutional governance.
Demonstration Approach

Teacher Support Program

Multiple Locations

A targeted, peer-led professional development model that equips public school educators with the precise instructional toolkits and mental health frameworks needed to manage complex classrooms.
Community Leadership

Fale Fale Shiksha Muhim

Gogunda, Rajasthan

A localized, doorstep-delivery learning model that maps, trains, and deploys neighborhood youth to bridge foundational literacy deficits right inside children's homes.
Community Leadership

Learning Festivals Internship

Multiple Locations

A short-term immersion track where outside talent pairs with local youth to run intensive, project-based camps focused on children's practical reasoning and self-expression.
Systems Excellence

STAR Parents

Adopted by MCD, All Regions

A structural intervention that organizes and coaches parents to actively run School Management Committees, establishing a permanent system of civic oversight and at-home learning routines.

Our Journey

The Story So Far

Ten years ago, Kshamtalaya began with a simple belief: every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive, regardless of their circumstances. Today, we work alongside communities, educators, and institutions to strengthen learning ecosystems and create pathways toward a more equitable future.

2016

The beginning, in Kotra

Two fellows in a remote tribal block, asking what real education would look like there. Incorporated as a Section 8 company on 27 July 2016.

2017

First programmes

Learning Festivals begin in June. The iDISCOVER Fellowship inducts its first cohort of grassroots education leaders in November.

2018

Into Delhi

Work begins in MCD government schools in East Delhi, expanding our urban footprint.

2019

Curriculum with the state

SEE Learning launched with Emory University and the Dalai Lama Trust. Entrepreneurial Mindset Curriculum co-created with SCERT Delhi.

2020

The pandemic & radio

Relief for families in Kotra. Radio learning launched with Radio Madhuban for children with no internet access.

2021

Institutional footing

CSR registration approved in April 2021, opening the door to transformative corporate partnerships.

2022

Into Bihar

Work begins in Samastipur district, scaling impact in partnership with SCERT Bihar.

2023

Recognised globally

The parent engagement model reaches the global Top 10 for the World's Best School Prize for Community Collaboration.

2025

Adopted by the system

The STAR Parents model is officially adopted by the Municipal Corporation of Delhi starting 1 April 2025.

2026

The depth decade begins

Ten years in. An Advisory Board constituted. The next decade is about deeper roots and stronger institutions.
